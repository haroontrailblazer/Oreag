# Oreag — Context loop logo concept

Generated with the built-in image generation tool and approved for Oreag. This is a raster concept, not a vector master. Its transparent symbol is now used throughout the app; see README.md in this folder for the asset pipeline.

Three broad connected forms surround a central opening, suggesting knowledge retained and recalled. A solid monochrome silhouette and a bold Oreag wordmark keep the identity simple.

Product context was verified against the current project README: document retrieval, grounded answers, APIs, and persistent agent memory. The user's requested references were Codex, Claude, and OpenAI, used for visual simplicity and confidence rather than reproducing their marks.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Design an outstanding ORIGINAL logo for "Oreag", an AI knowledge, retrieval and persistent memory platform.

The user wants the visual confidence and simplicity of the Codex, Claude and OpenAI identities: a memorable standalone symbol, substantial strokes, intelligent negative space, monochrome impact. Use those as high-level quality references only. Create a distinct new shape; do not reproduce their logos, the OpenAI six-loop knot, the Claude sunburst, or the Codex mark.

Art direction: a beautiful compact abstract "context loop" formed from THREE broad, softly squared folded bands that appear to connect around one clean central negative-space aperture. The exterior silhouette is approximately circular with subtle rounded-square character. The three bands have precise, rhythmic cuts and one purposeful offset that gives the mark character and a sense of returning to retained knowledge. Bold graphic weight, broad clear gaps, disciplined proportions, highly legible at app-icon size. No letter R, no OR monogram, no camera shutter blades, no recycling arrows, no tiny details, no literal brain, no document icon, no circuit diagram, no nodes, no mascot. It must feel designed by a leading identity studio and be simple enough to draw from memory. Favor an elegant distinctive silhouette over complexity.

Output: ONE finished logo composition on a completely opaque, flat, pure white (#FFFFFF) square canvas. White background is mandatory. Render the logo in completely opaque solid black (#111111) with NO shadows, NO glow, NO gray texture, NO gradients, NO bevels, NO outlines. Strict two-dimensional flat vector-like artwork with crisp edges and flawless curves.
Center the large standalone symbol in the upper-middle of the canvas, about 38% of canvas width. Beneath it, leave generous space and place the exact word "Oreag" (O-r-e-a-g) in a refined bold geometric sans-serif, elegantly kerned, approximately 32% of canvas width. Make the wordmark visually subordinate to the symbol. Plenty of surrounding white negative space.
No presentation grid, no secondary panels, no mockups, no commentary text, no slogans, no color swatches, no watermarks. A strong real logo, not a brand guidelines poster.
